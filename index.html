Zero conditional

If you heat ice, it melts.

  • One thing (it, the light, a car): add -s it melts
  • You, we, they, many things: no -s they melt
__MQ__melt __MQ__melts __MQ__freezes

How to play

  • Every group has a lane. Each lane has three mosquitoes with three endings.
  • Tap the right ending. A correct catch scores 1 point.
  • First group to catch it gets 2 bonus points.
  • Wrong mosquito? Your lane freezes for 2 seconds.
